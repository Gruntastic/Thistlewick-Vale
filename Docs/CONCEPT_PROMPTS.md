## H. UI look notes (for the UI engineer; no generation needed)
- **Mood**: warm hearth wood and wheat parchment, framed by dark bark; glow accents in Ember Gold. Same low-poly faceted language: chamfered corners (45 degree cuts, not rounded), flat fills, one-step darker bevel line, no gradients or photo textures, no thin lines under 2 px.
- **Panel**: Dark Bark (#4A3428) fill at 90% opacity, 3 px Timber (#7F5539) border, 1 px Ember Gold inner highlight on the top edge, chamfered 6 px corners. Title bar Timber, text Wheat.
- **Button**: normal Timber fill with Wheat text; hover Clay fill + Ember Gold 2 px border; pressed Dark Bark fill; disabled Slate fill, Stone Grey text. Primary action button Hearth Red fill, Bone text.
- **Frames** (unit frames, action bar slots, item slots): square slots with chamfered corners, Dark Bark fill, 2 px border color by rarity (Common Stone Grey, Uncommon Moss Green, Rare Dusk Blue, Epic Hollow Violet). Selection/ready glow in Ember Gold.
- **Bars**: health Moss Green (hostile Hearth Red), mana Dusk Blue, Rage Hearth Red, Focus Meadow Lime; cast bar Ember Gold (interruptible) or Stone Grey (uninterruptible); track Dark Bark.
- **Icons** (128 px): single chunky object centered on a flat Wheat or tier-colored disc, 8 px safe margin, flat fills from the palette, one darker facet side for volume, no outline thinner than 3 px, glow limited to a soft outer halo for T3 (Ember Gold). Ability icons: class accent color background.
- **Font**: a rounded slab or sturdy serif with generous weight (engineer picks from engine-available fonts); minimum 14 px body, 12 px absolute minimum.
